---
name: Conference community policy
description: Organizer's durable preferences for attendee participation, prizes, and moderation
---

Keep community participation tied to the name attendees already enter in the app; do not require registration-email access, a shared conference code, or a separate verification step. Device transfer and recovery are not a priority for this conference.

**Why:** Many attendees are registered by their employers and do not receive registration email. The organizer explicitly prioritizes ease of participation over stronger identity verification and considers device changes unlikely.

**How to apply:** Preserve the low-friction join flow when extending photo comments, Discussion, and prize participation. Do not add login or recovery friction without a new request.

Keep a named leaderboard visible during the conference, favor conversation in scoring, and let posts appear immediately while organizers handle reports afterward.

**Why:** The organizer wants visible competition to encourage engagement, but expects a professional audience and prefers moderation controls as a safety net rather than a preapproval queue.

**How to apply:** Changes to ranking, reporting, or moderation should preserve public standings and should not hide content merely because someone reported it; moderators decide whether to hide content.
